Privacy Policy

App Name: Editor Magic

Effective Date: 26 September 2026

Editor Magic respects your privacy. This application is designed primarily as an offline image editor, video editor, sticker maker, and WhatsApp sticker exporter. Most media processing happens entirely on your device.

We do not require user registration or collect personal information such as:

  • Name

The app may access:

  • Photos and images

We do not upload your media to our servers.
