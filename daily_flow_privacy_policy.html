Privacy Policy

Daily Routines & Habit Tracker

Last Updated: October 31, 2026

Thank you for choosing DayFlow ("Daily Routines & Habit Tracker", "we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal data you generate while using our app. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding your information.

1. Offline-First Philosophy

DayFlow is built with an offline-first design architecture:

  • Your daily routines, habits, tasks, expenses, notes, and wellness records are stored exclusively in a secure SQLite database on your local device.
  • We do not maintain remote company servers that collect, inspect, monetize, or store your personal routine entries or financial notes.

2. Information We Handle

A. On-Device Data (Stored Locally Only)

  • Habits, routine schedules, checklists, notes, expenses, streaks, and breathing/wellness logs.
  • User interface settings, including theme preference (Light/Dark mode) and optional 4-digit App Lock PIN.
  • This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly initiate an export or cloud backup.

B. Cloud Backup via Google Drive (Optional & User-Initiated)

  • If you choose to enable Google Drive Backup, you will authenticate using Google Sign-In (google_sign_in).
  • The app requests access strictly to its own application data folder (drive.appdata scope) to store your encrypted or serialized backup JSON file.
  • We do not access, view, or manage your personal Google Drive documents, photos, or emails.
  • You can disconnect your Google account at any time in the Settings screen.

C. Advertising (Google AdMob)

  • DayFlow may display non-intrusive advertisements powered by Google Mobile Ads SDK.
  • Google AdMob may process non-personally identifiable technical information (such as advertising ID, general device model, and crash logs) to serve relevant or non-personalized ads according to Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

3. App Security & Permissions

  • Notifications: DayFlow requests notification permission to trigger scheduled routine and habit reminders locally on your device.
  • Local Storage Access: Used only to save offline backup JSON files when you request an export.
  • App Lock: If you configure a 4-digit security PIN, the PIN is saved locally on your device using encrypted shared preferences.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Since your data is stored locally, uninstalling the DayFlow app will permanently delete all local databases and preferences from your device.
  • If you backed up your data to Google Drive, you can delete the backup file anytime through the App settings or by managing Google Drive app data directly in your Google account settings.

5. Children's Privacy

DayFlow does not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 13. The application is a general-purpose self-improvement and organization tool.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any revisions will be reflected with an updated "Last Updated" date at the top of this document and will be immediately accessible within the application settings.
